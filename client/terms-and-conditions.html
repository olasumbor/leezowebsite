TERMS & CONDITIONS

The border service of any agency maintains the right to open and inspect any package from a shipment. LEEZOFOODNG.EXPORT is not responsible for any item removed, opened, or qualified unfit. We are not responsible for any delay in transit and it is beyond our control.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

  1. Shopping services are not free, this attracts a fee.
  2. Pick up charges shall be based on the location of your cargo within Lagos.
  3. Goods brought in for shipping shall be thoroughly checked and searched by our staff.
  4. The airlines shall decide the final weight of the package and whatever weight they indicate put on the airway bill shall be what is due for payment.
  5. We shall not accept any contrabands. Examples are Pomo, meat, poultry products, dairy products, drugs, alcohol, dried vegetables.
  6. We shall not be responsible for customs checks, removal of items, customs seizures, etc., as we do not communicate with the customs of any country.
  7. Payment shall validate orders and shipments.
  8. Your pickup shall attract customs clearance charges depending on the country.
  9. All items should be packed in transparent ziplock bags as repackaging of goods shall attract a fee.
  10. Shipping dates shall be estimated and not final as circumstances beyond our control may arise at the port or with the airline.
  11. Once goods have been airlifted from Nigeria, Leezofoodng.export shall not accept any liabilities for airline delays, customs seizure, fines, or destruction of goods.

We thank you for your patronage.
